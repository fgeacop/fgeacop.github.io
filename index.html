Our purpose

Building capability for a changing world

We create a trusted platform for exchanging innovative ideas and practical approaches to modern enterprise architecture.

Participation is voluntary and by invitation, bringing together people with deep relevance to the discipline and practice of enterprise architecture. Through knowledge sharing, professional development and capacity building, we explore emerging technologies, their opportunities and their risks.

01
