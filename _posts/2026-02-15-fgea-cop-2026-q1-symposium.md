The FGEA CoP 2026 Q1 Symposium was scheduled as an online event on Thursday 26 March 2026, from 10:00 am to 12:30 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** “Human-centric AI for Software Development: Opportunities and Challenges”, Professor John Grundy, Monash University; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **10:40:** “AI architecture at the appropriate models”, Colin Renouf; chaired by Dr Michelle Graham, Department of Home Affairs.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** “Semantic Layer for Enterprise Architecture”, Dr Madhushi Bandara, University of Technology Sydney.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “Semantic Search with LLM Embeddings: Beyond Keywords to Meaning”, Dr Chong Teck Ng, DJCS; chaired by Dr Madhushi Bandara.
