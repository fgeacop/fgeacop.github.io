The FGEA CoP Q3 Symposium was successfully held on Thursday 25 September 2025 as a hybrid event at ACS Sydney and online. The day began with a light morning tea at 9:30 am and concluded at approximately 1:00 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Helen Warner, ACS, Professor Asif Gill, and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** “Trustworthy Systems: Machine learning a challenge for Enterprise Systems”, Professor Boris Düdder, University of Copenhagen, Denmark; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **10:40:** “Adaptive EA for Augmented Intelligence”, Professor Asif Gill, UTS DigiSAS Lab; chaired by Vesna Andric, DTA.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** “AI Infrastructure Sovereignty”, Justin Stark, Accenture; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “Future of AI enabled Enterprise Architecture: Demos and Discussions”, Bushra Al Sulayyim, Grace Billiris, and Eesha Oaj, UTS; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
