The FGEA CoP Q2 Symposium was successfully held on Thursday 26 June 2025 as a hybrid event at the UTS RES Hub and online. The day began with a light morning tea at 9:30 am and concluded at approximately 1:00 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** “Enterprise, Systems and Software Reference Architectures”, Associate Professor Peter Bernus, Griffith University; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **10:40:** “Establishing efficient AI risk management”, Daniel Roelink, Department of Customer Service; chaired by Vanessa Douglas-Savage, DTA.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** “Accelerating Digital and AI Transformation in Higher Education: A Customer-Driven Approach”, Galen White, FromHereOn; chaired by Margaret Codner, UTS.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “Scalable Enterprise AI Systems and Operating Model: Architecture Co-pilot”, Dr Terry Roach, Capsifi/Orbus; chaired by Darryl Carr, HCA Advisory, and Professor Asif Gill, UTS.
