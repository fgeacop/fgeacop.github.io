The FGEA CoP Q3 Symposium was announced as a hybrid event at KPMG Sydney and online for Thursday 26 September 2024, from 9:30 am to 1:30 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **9:30:** Arrival, registration, and coffee or tea for in-person guests.
- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill, UTS, and Mark Tims, Partner, KPMG. The published agenda also lists Tom Marwick and Lindsay Smith, DoH.
- **10:10:** Invited talk on digital health data in the United States, Professor Ophir Frieder, Georgetown University; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **10:40:** “Formulating digital strategies”, Dr Michelle Graham, Department of Home Affairs; chaired by Janina Harper, Director, KPMG.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** Panel discussion, “Navigating through data & digital challenges”, with Professor Erik Proper, TU Wien, Vienna; Matt Boughton, Head of Architecture, WNSW; Felicity Colbourne, Digital Transformation Director, KPMG; and David Ryan, Partner, KPMG; chaired by Janina Harper.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “The role of architects in delivering transformational outcomes”, Ray Ouyang, Director, Federal Government Technology Advisory Lead, KPMG; chaired by Janina Harper.
- **12:30:** Networking with a light lunch for in-person guests.
