The FGEA CoP 2026 Q2 Symposium was scheduled as an online event on Thursday 25 June 2026, from 10:00 am to 12:30 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Dr Madhushi Bandara and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** “Architecture in the age of probabilistic software”, Luca Mezzalira, 50 Cents Media Ltd, UK; chaired by Dr Madhushi Bandara, UTS.
- **10:40:** “Architecting Human-Centric AI Systems”, Professor Asif Gill, UTS.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** “Architecture as Code: Applying architectural constraints in the era of AI Augmented software and digital product delivery”, Tim Stokes, Ticked.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “AI Operating Model”, Dr Alan Thorogood, MIT CISR; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
