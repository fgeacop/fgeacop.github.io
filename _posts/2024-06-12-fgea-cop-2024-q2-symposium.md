The FGEA CoP Q2 Symposium was successfully held on Thursday 27 June 2024, from 9:30 am to 1:30 pm. Australian Computer Society hosted the hybrid event at its Barangaroo boardroom and online. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **9:30:** Arrival, registration, and coffee or tea.
- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and the ACS chair.
- **10:10:** “Data governance is a journey not a destination”, Kate Carruthers, Chief Data & Insights Officer, UNSW.
- **10:40:** “Digital operations architecture”, Matthew Foy, Director Digital Operations Architecture, NSW Department of Education.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** Panel discussion on how organisations can manage architecture data, with Dr Terry Roach, Founder and Chief Innovation Officer, Capsifi; Dr Seyran Dehbokry, Associate Director, Technology Advisory, KPMG; Awais Malik, Pre-sales Manager APAC, Orbus Software; Craig Stanley, Head of EA Alliances and Head of Enterprise Architecture, Kapish; and Liam Ryan, Strategy and Operations APAC, Avolution. The published questions addressed:
  1. what constitutes relevant architecture data across diverse enterprise and ecosystem applications, services, and capabilities;
  2. how to collect, source, link, maintain, and discover architecture data across repositories; and
  3. the main impediments to, and recommendations for, architecture data collection and management, including cost, value, and data quality.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “Get on with responsible AI and trusted data”, Professor Liming Zhu, Research Director, Software and Computational Systems, CSIRO’s Data61.
- **12:30–1:30:** Networking with a light lunch.
