## Published agenda

- **9:30:** Arrival, registration, and coffee or tea for in-person guests.
- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** “Future generation human-centric and decision-driven AI system architecture and engineering”, Professor Asif Gill, University of Technology Sydney.
- **10:40:** “UTS Technology Roadmap: more than just shelfware”, John Keaveny and Seema Raghav, enterprise architects, UTS; chaired by Margaret Codner.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** “Data & Digital 2024 and beyond”, with Professor Ian Oppermann, Distinguished Professor Fang Chen, Keith Sherringham, and David White; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “Architecture as a decision science”, Michael Pascoe, Chief Enterprise Architect, Murdoch University; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **12:30:** Networking with a light lunch for in-person guests.
