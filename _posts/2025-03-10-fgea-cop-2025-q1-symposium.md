The FGEA CoP Q1 Symposium was announced as a fully online event for Thursday 27 March 2025, from 10:00 am to 12:30 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** “Composable Architecture & Data Modernisation: Navigating the future of AI for business value”, Mark Sanders, Chief Architect, Telstra, and Senthoor Punniamoorthy, Principal Architect, Telstra; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **10:40:** “EduChat architecture patterns”, Vincent Lui, Solution Architect, NSW Education; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** Conversation on AI strategy and architecture acceleration, with Professor Asif Gill, Dr Christine Stepheson, and Alan Hsiao.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “How Can We Architect Gen-AI? Strategies, Capabilities & Governance”, Conor Farrell, APAC Product Consultant, Avolution; chaired by Darryl Carr, HCA Advisory.
