The FGEA CoP Q4 Symposium was successfully held on Thursday 4 December 2025 as a hybrid event at the University of Technology Sydney and online. The day began with a light morning tea at 9:30 am and concluded at approximately 1:00 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and Dr Vanessa Douglas-Savage, FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** “Evolution of the Australian Government Architecture”, Dr Vanessa Douglas-Savage, Digital Transformation Agency; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
- **10:40:** “Tech Consolidation Feasibility Assessments: Lessons Learnt”, Eaglan Kurek, UTS Enterprise Architecture Unit; chaired by Margaret Codner, Head of Architecture and Design, UTS.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** “Designing the Future Organisation: How AI, Human-Centred Design and Architecture Intersect to Move Organisations Forward at Speed”, Fran Mether, Arteri; chaired by Dr Seyran Dehbokry, KPMG.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** “Advances in Optical Communication Systems: 2025 John Tyndall Award Lecture”, Professor Masatoshi Suzuki, Chitose Institute of Science and Technology; chaired by Professor Asif Gill.
